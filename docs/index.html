Gremlin the Friend · setup guide for Windows 11

A friendly gremlin that lives in your PC.

You talk to it on Signal. It keeps your calendar and to-do list and reminds you of things. It only answers you, it's never exposed to the internet, and your calendar and tasks are stored on your own devices.

About 2 hours in total, most of it waiting for installs. Claude Code does the typing; you do the clicking. Free and open source on GitHub.

Be clear-eyed

What stays private, and what doesn't

Your gremlin is private from the internet and from strangers. Your conversations go to whichever brain you choose. Only the local brain keeps them on your PC.

Stays on your devicesYour calendar and tasks (if kept on your PC, the default), the gremlin's notes about you, its settings and Signal keys, and your backups unless you sync them to Dropbox or OneDrive.
Goes to your brainEvery message you send, plus whatever the gremlin reads to answer it: events, tasks, notes. With a local brain, that stays on your PC. With a cloud brain, see the ratings below.
Passes through, encryptedSignal carries your messages end-to-end encrypted. Tailscale connects your devices, and the traffic between them is encrypted end to end.
Local, on your PC●●●●●Never leaves your PC. Free. Needs a strong PC (ideally an NVIDIA card with 8 GB+); slower and less capable than the cloud.
Ollama Cloud●●●○○Says it processes and discards prompts, no training. Mostly US servers. Free with limits, or $20 a month.
Claude (Anthropic)●●○○○Most capable. Kept 30 days with model training off; up to 5 years and used for training if on. Claude Pro subscription.

There's no silent fallback: if your brain fails, you get an error, not a less private brain. Claude Code is only the installer; it sees the setup, not your daily chats.

Calendar and tasks for the bot

The script asks where your calendar should live. If you're not sure, choose 1. You can switch later by running it again.

1 · On your PCNothing to sign up for. Syncs while the PC is awake; your phone works offline in between. No email invitations.Free · default

Lock it down and back it up

This switches off features you don't need, gives the bot its rulebook, makes its files private, and saves a backup every night. Point the backup at a Dropbox or OneDrive folder to get a copy off the PC.

Ubuntu
bash 06-harden.sh "/mnt/c/Users/YOU/Dropbox/openclaw-backups"
  1. If your brain is Claude: claude.ai → Settings → Data privacy controls, turn model training off.
  2. Windows Security → Device security → Core isolation: turn Memory Integrity on.
  3. BitLocker (encrypts your disk): search "Manage BitLocker" in Start and turn it on. Save the recovery key in your password manager. The PC is slower until the first encryption finishes.
Keep it private

The rules for keeping a gremlin

Never feed it passwordsNot in a chat with your gremlin, with Claude Code, or with any AI. If one slips through, change that password.
Keep it out of the sunlightIt stays off the open internet. Only your own devices can reach it, over Tailscale.
Don't let strangers talk to itIt answers only your number, and groups are switched off.
Don't steal its mailEach Signal message can be picked up only once. Never run signal-cli receive yourself.
It can't delete without your yesBefore it removes an event or task, you confirm that specific item.
If something goes wrong

Troubleshooting

Tell Claude Code what you see. It has the full list in GUIDE.md. These are the usual suspects.

The bot doesn't answer at all

Double-click Start OpenClaw on your Desktop and wait a minute. If the shortcut says "down" but the bot replies, trust the bot. If it's still silent, run this in an admin PowerShell:

Start-ScheduledTask -TaskName "OpenClaw Keepalive"
The bot's phone shows my message, but the bot never answers

Something else picked up the message first, usually a manual signal-cli receive. Stop it and restart the assistant:

systemctl --user restart openclaw-gateway

Don't re-link the device. That's almost never the fix.

The bot says a tool or option doesn't exist

It's still using an old conversation. Send /new in Signal.

Tasks.org: "CLEARTEXT communication not permitted"

The server address must start with https:// and end in .ts.net/. Use the exact address step 8 printed, not a number like 100.x.x.x.

Tasks.org: "connection abort", spinning, or error 500, after it used to work

First, check your PC is awake and the bot answers. If it doesn't, follow "The bot doesn't answer at all". Then open the Tailscale app on your phone. If it shows missing devices or "out of sync", sign out and back in. Also check Always-on VPN is on, and Private DNS is Off or Automatic.

The gremlin is very slow, or answers with nonsense

With a local brain on a PC without a strong graphics card, slow replies are expected. If it ignores its tools or replies with raw code, the model is too small for the job. Run bash brain.sh again and pick a bigger model or a cloud brain.

Step 9 says 401, or the bot can't reach my calendar

The login was refused. It's usually the account's main password instead of an app password, or a typo in the email. Make a new app password and run bash 05-plugins.sh again.

A window keeps flashing on screen

A background task was set up without its "hidden" setting. Run step 4's script again in an admin PowerShell.

"Access is denied" in PowerShell

That window isn't an administrator window. Close it, right-click PowerShell, and choose Run as administrator.

"Sudo is disabled on this machine" or "parameter 'fsSL'"

A Linux command went into PowerShell. Run it in the Ubuntu window instead. Paste there with a right-click.
